# OSDI Range Composition Boundary

## Purpose and Scope

This component owns model-local conditional identity capture and records the
unclosed physical constitutive composition boundary for PR20. Its parent is
[CoreSpiceOSDI](../DESIGN.md); it has no children. Controlled actual-bound capture
is qualified by the finite Native tests below; no callable physical range/root
consumer or all-history certificate follows.

The declared public SDK dependency is `swift-osdi` 1.2.0 at
`e17d3bcca1224c8ab9ccfaddea7a47580b29b748`. The exact public pin and ordinary
consumer adoption are owned by the parent design. Earlier 1.0.1/1.1.0 evidence
retains its original source, worker and finite input scope.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries

The SDK owns source-program interpretation, exact setup/topology, source branch
semantics and outward constitutive evaluation. CoreSpice must own circuit-level
projection, retained body/internal nodes, algebraic reconstruction, exact external
charge, validated physical transient tubes and source-flow composition. Timing
owns the final source/history/event/replay admission. No finite point trace or
smooth constitutive box substitutes for these proofs.

### Equation Transformation Is Not a Physical Chart

Exact null-charge rows L and an invertible equation-row transformation [B; L]
are lower facts only. They do not prove that `(B*Q(x)-q, L*F(x,u))=0` has a
unique inverse in the full MNA coordinate vector. The actual compiler retains
voltage-source branch currents. For a capacitor parallel to a grounded prescribed
voltage source, x=(v,i), Q=(C*v,0), F=(i,v-u), L=(0,1) and B=(1,0).
The row transform is the identity, yet `(C*v-q,v-u)` is independent of i.
The original DAE instead determines i=-C*u'. A source-current equation cannot be
silently removed or replaced by an independent local `F=0` assertion.

Physical reconstruction therefore requires a separate exact reduction of
prescribed-voltage constraints, a justified independent charge coordinate set,
and reconstruction of all original branch currents from the complete KCL and
input derivatives. Its chain rule includes `Q_u*u'`, with one-sided derivatives
and charge compatibility at source seams. Ideal-source loops, missing derivative
authority and unresolved algebraic branches must remain explicit refusals or
complete retained branches. Neither a sampled Jacobian rank nor successful exact
row completion closes this obligation.

The falsifier follows the production `MatrixTopology`, `BoundVoltageSource` and
`BoundCapacitor` paths. Future physical-chart tests must include this circuit,
nonconstant prescribed input, full original KCL replay and source seam behavior.

### Resource Admission and Cleanup

walks and copies use bounded, precharged batches and cancellation checkpoints.
